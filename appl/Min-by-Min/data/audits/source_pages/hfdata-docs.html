Methodology

Data sources

Source: IEX Exchange market data. Every bar is parsed directly from IEX Exchange HIST TOPS pcap files (IEX's top-of-book feed) into one-minute OHLCV bars. IEX represents approximately 2–3% of consolidated volume, so these bars capture IEX-only activity, not the full tape.

Data provided for free by IEX. By accessing or using IEX Historical Data, you agree to the IEX Historical Data Terms of Use.

Every file carries a source column; every bar is iex.

Eight-step cleaning pipeline

The Clean version applies the following pipeline. Each step is applied in order. A bar that fails any step is removed.

Step Rule Rationale
1 Remove bars outside 09:30–16:00 ET Pre/post-market bars have different microstructure properties
2 Remove bars with non-positive prices (Open, High, Low, or Close ≤ 0) Clearly erroneous
3 Remove bars where High < Low OHLC violation — impossible in valid trade data
4 Remove bars where Open or Close falls outside [Low, High] OHLC violation — Open and Close must be within the bar's range
5 Remove duplicate timestamps (keep first occurrence) Source data occasionally contains exact duplicates
6 Remove bars with zero volume No trade occurred — price is stale or indicative
7 Remove bars where |log return| > 25% (relative to previous close) Extreme outlier filter — returns exceeding 25% in one minute are almost certainly errors
8 Brownlees-Gallo filter: remove bars where |close − median| > 3 × MAD, computed over a 50-bar centered window Adaptive outlier detection that accounts for local price level

Bars removed: 9,263,399 (about 3% of raw). The cleaning is conservative by design.

Coverage

  • 1,253 tickers — U.S. equities and ETFs
  • Begin March 7, 2022 — IEX Exchange HIST data only
  • Updated daily — new bars added each trading day via automated pipeline
  • 09:30–16:00 Eastern Time only — the same window on every day, early-close days included (see Known Issues)
  • Adjusted prices — prices and volumes are adjusted for stock splits and for nothing else. Dividends and spin-offs are not removed: on an ex-date a price falls as it did in the market, so every return is a price return (see Known Issues)

Universe construction & survivorship

The ticker list is not reconstructed point-in-time: names are not added or removed automatically as companies list, delist, merge, or fail. Each ticker's series starts at its first bar and ends at its last one.

⚠ The ticker list is not point-in-time.

It includes names that traded on IEX in the covered period and later delisted (e.g. SVB Financial, First Republic), so delisted names are not simply dropped. But names are not added automatically as companies list, so a cross-sectional study should check that the tickers it needs are present.

Guidance:

  • Suitable for: time-series and intraday microstructure analysis of the listed tickers, and data-cleaning/methodology research.
  • Use with caution for: cross-sectional studies that need recently listed companies, which the fixed universe does not add.
  • Separate from survivorship: the IEX-only volume caveat noted under Data sources above (≈2–3% of consolidated volume) also applies.

A point-in-time, delisting-inclusive universe (built from CRSP/TAQ) is a known future direction, not part of the current release.

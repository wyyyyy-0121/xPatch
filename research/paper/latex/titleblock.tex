{\Large\bfseries Intraday Realized-Variance Forecasting for AAPL: A Reproducible
Comparison of Regularized Regression, Trees, and Time-Series Models}\par
\vspace{5pt}
{\normalsize Author information to be added}\par
{\small Working manuscript -- 4 November 2026}\par
\end{center}
\begin{center}\begin{minipage}{0.96\textwidth}\small
\textbf{Abstract.} We compare regularized regression, gradient-boosted trees, and
time-series models for forecasting AAPL's realized variance over the
next 30 minutes. Using SIP minute OHLCV data from January 2021 through
June 2026, we evaluate an AAPL-only information set and a market
information set that also includes SPY and QQQ history. The design
combines ten quarterly expanding-window evaluations, three inner
temporal validation folds, separate calibration windows, and
whole-session purge gaps. The primary score is session-weighted QLIKE on
the variance scale. Candidate qualification additionally requires
dependence-aware inference, Holm adjustment across ten comparisons, and
quarterly, annual-segment, and seed stability. The development sample
contains 602 eligible sessions and 207,235 forecast origins per model.
Ridge reduces QLIKE relative to the corresponding HAR baseline by 7.74\%
and 7.41\% in the two information sets and meets all specified
development gates. A subsequent historical confirmation sample of 251
sessions yields improvements of 6.29\% and 7.97\%. The evaluated xPatch
and fixed-weight Chronos-2 configurations do not meet the joint gates.
AAPL-only LightGBM improves aggregate QLIKE but fails the annual and
seed stability requirements. These findings support retaining carefully
tuned regularized baselines for this particular task. The evidence
remains limited to one target asset, previously exposed historical
periods, and incompletely established pretraining contamination
boundaries. It does not establish timely prospective performance or
trading profitability.
\par\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{Keywords:} realized variance; intraday forecasting; HAR; Ridge; xPatch; temporal
validation
\end{minipage}\end{center}
